## The four top-level skills

> "AI allows us to build software very differently today than in 2022... But with the noisy, hype-filled, information environment around AI, what are the most valuable skills for you to learn?"
> — Andrew Ng, *The AI Engineering Skills Map*, 2026-08-14

The map was produced by analysing over 10,000 job postings, carrying out dozens of structured interviews with AI experts, hiring managers and recruiters, gathering survey data, and synthesising other online sources. Ng describes the process as akin to clustering on a large dataset of jobs and expert interviews.

| Part | Skill | Sub-skills | The core difference it addresses |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Building and deploying AI applications | 6 | AI output is **unpredictable**, so development must be highly iterative |
| 2 | Software engineering fundamentals | 5 | Determines whether you can even **see the trade-offs** that exist |
| 3 | Using coding agents | 5 | The fastest-evolving skill; the crux is how much to intervene |
| 4 | Shaping the build | 4 | Agents implement a spec well, so human value moves to **deciding the spec** |

**A note on terminology.** The subject is *AI Engineering skills*, not the "AI Engineer" role. The former is far broader — just as all developers need to work with the cloud while only some are titled Cloud Engineer, so full-stack, data, DevOps and ML engineers all need AI engineering skills.

**The mindset underneath all four.** Continuous learning. The map is expected to change; the durable asset is the habit of updating your own workflows.
